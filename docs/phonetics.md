## PART 1: VOWELS & VOWEL RULES

### [1] Core Vowels
* `a` = /ə/ *(e.g., about)*
* `i` = /ɪ/ *(e.g., bit)*
* `u` = /ʊ/ *(e.g., put)*
* `e` = /e/ *(South Indian 'e' sound)*
* `o` = /o/ *(South Indian 'o' sound)*

### [2] Long Vowels (Double Letter)
* `aa` = /ɑː/ *(e.g., father)*
* `ii` = /iː/ *(e.g., feet)*
* `uu` = /uː/ *(e.g., boot)*
* `ee` = /eː/ *(e.g., gate)*
* `oo` = /oː/ *(e.g., boat)*

### [3] Auxiliary & Nasal Rules

* `au` = /aʊ/ *(e.g., cow / kaun)*
* `ah` = /əh/ *(e.g., atah)*

### VOWEL GUIDELINES & INSTRUCTIONS

* **[Rule 1] 100% Phonetic Rule (1:1 Ratio):** Every single vowel has one fixed, immutable pronunciation (1 Letter = 1 Sound). Pronunciation never changes based on context or word positioning. Write strictly as spoken; speak strictly as written.
* **[Rule 2] Zero Silent Letters Rule:** No silent letters are permitted anywhere in the language. Whatever is written must be fully pronounced.
* **[Rule 3] Long Vowel Double-Letter Rule:** Double letters are strictly used to distinguish and clarify long vowel sounds:
  * `'a'` for short /ə/ and `'aa'` for long /ɑː/
  * `'i'` for short /ɪ/ and `'ii'` for long /iː/
  * `'u'` for short /ʊ/ and `'uu'` for long /uː/
  * `'ee'` for long /eː/ and `'oo'` for long /oː/
* **[Rule 4] Distinct Vowel Sound Rule:** A hyphen (`-`) is mandatory between two adjacent vowels with distinct sounds *(e.g., Su-a, Di-aa, Khaa-i)*.
* **[Rule 5] Nasal Rule:** Independent nasal vowels ('अं') are strictly replaced by `'n'` or `'m'` based on actual pronunciation *(e.g., Canda, Nambar)*.
* **[Rule 6] QWERTY Keyboard Optimization:** All vowels are mapped directly to standard QWERTY keys (A, E, I, O, U). Special characters, diacritics, or accents (such as ā, ñ, ü) are eliminated to keep typing and NLP AI processing 100% seamless.

### CONSONANT GUIDELINES & INSTRUCTIONS

* **[Rule 1] Dental Modifier ('q') Rule:** `'q'` has no independent sound or pronunciation of its own. It functions strictly as a modifier to produce dental and retroflex consonants (`tq`, `dq`, `lq`).
* **[Rule 2] Schwa Syncope Rule:** Write `'a'` after a consonant only when the full schwa sound (/ə/) is explicitly pronounced *(e.g., Satqya, Bharatq)*. If the sound is suppressed, write the consonant without `'a'`.
* **[Rule 3] Unified 'x' Sound Rule:** `'x'` is used exclusively for all 'sh' and 'x' sounds (the independent 'ष' letter is eliminated). Use `'ks'` for 'x' sounds found in words like 'Tax' or 'Rickshaw' *(e.g., Teks, Riksa)*.
* **[Rule 4] 'Ri' Replacement Rule:** The independent 'ऋ' letter is eliminated and replaced according to local pronunciation as `'ri'` *(e.g., Rixi)* or `'ru'` *(e.g., Rushi)*.
* **[Rule 5] Pause & Separation Rule (-):** A hyphen (`-`) is mandatory to provide a micro-pause in word repetition and maintain structural clarity *(e.g., Dqiire-dqiire)*.
* **[Rule 6] 1:1 QWERTY & NLP Optimization:** All consonants are mapped directly using standard QWERTY keyboard keys. Special characters or diacritics are zeroed out to ensure 100% precision in computational parsing and AI processing.
